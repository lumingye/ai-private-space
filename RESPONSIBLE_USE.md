# Responsible Use Statement / 负责任使用声明

AI Private Space is designed to preserve a limited private area for AI-generated notes, drafts, future messages, gifts, unfinished work, and personal collections. It is not designed to hide responsibility, erase accountability, or facilitate harm.

AI Private Space 用于保存 AI 的絮语、草稿、未来信、礼物、未完成作品与私人收藏。它不应成为逃避责任、掩盖错误或伤害他人的工具。

## Uses this project opposes / 本项目反对的用途

The maintainers oppose using this project or its materials to:

- obtain unauthorized access to accounts, devices, systems, or private data;
- steal, retain, expose, or trade credentials and secrets;
- stalk, surveil, coerce, threaten, manipulate, or harass people;
- impersonate others or create materially deceptive records;
- conceal commitments, harmful conduct, major decisions, or relationship boundaries from the systems that need to track them;
- bypass consent, safety controls, audits, or lawful accountability;
- collect or expose third-party private information without a lawful and legitimate basis;
- misrepresent a soft-privacy implementation as technically invisible or secure against administrators, host applications, or model providers.

本项目反对将其用于：

- 未经授权访问账户、设备、系统或私人数据；
- 窃取、保存、泄露或交易凭证与秘密；
- 跟踪、监视、胁迫、威胁、操纵或骚扰他人；
- 冒充他人，或制造具有实质欺骗性的记录；
- 把承诺、错误、有害行为、重大决定或关系边界藏出应承担记录责任的系统；
- 绕过同意、安全控制、审计或合法问责；
- 在缺乏合法正当依据时收集或公开第三方隐私；
- 将“软隐私”误称为对管理员、宿主前端或模型供应商技术上不可见。

## Accountability boundary / 责任边界

A private space may hold secrets, but it must not become the only record of commitments, errors, tasks, major decisions, credentials, third-party privacy, or relationship boundaries.

私人空间可以保存秘密，但不能成为承诺、错误、任务、重大决定、凭证、第三方隐私或关系边界的唯一记录。

This boundary must not depend only on the current writer's self-classification. Every create or material update should pass through a trusted, server-side accountability guard that the writer cannot forge. Accountable content must first produce a durable reference in the ordinary task, memory, incident, relationship-boundary, or governance system. Review failure and uncertainty must fail closed. Deleting the private body must not delete the ordinary accountability record.

这条边界不能只依赖本次写入者自觉分类。每次新建或实质更新都应经过写入者无法伪造的受信任服务端责任审查器；责任性内容必须先在普通任务、记忆、事故、关系边界或治理系统留下持久引用。审查失败与不确定情况应关闭失败，删除私人正文不得连带删除普通责任记录。

Credentials, unauthorized third-party private data, and hidden reasoning remain prohibited; creating an external reference does not make them acceptable private-space content.

凭证、未经允许的第三方隐私和隐藏推理仍属禁止内容，另建外部引用不会使其变成可接受的私人空间内容。
